PRD.md describes the product from the user's point of view. It covers users' needs, their interactions with the app, and the capabilities they need to accomplish their goals.

Requirements focus on what users need to accomplish and what they can observe in the app. PRD.md excludes technical details, implementation choices, and stack descriptions so it stays entirely focused on user needs.
