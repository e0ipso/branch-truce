Using the pack

FolderUse
svg/Editable vector paths. Choose for-light, for-dark, mono-black, or mono-white.
png/Ready-to-use raster images. The filename gives width in pixels.
platform/linux/App icons from 16 to 1024 px, scalable SVG, and symbolic SVG.
web/Favicons, Apple touch icon, regular and maskable app icons, and webmanifest.
social/Light and dark 1200 × 630 sharing images.
source/Master SVG, outlined components, approved reference, and rebuild scripts.

The full, horizontal, stacked, wordmark, and symbol versions have transparent backgrounds. Files in backgrounds/ have a colored canvas. Square and circle versions carry their own fields.

Size, spacing, and source notes

Suggested minimum widths: full 220 px, horizontal 320 px, stacked 200 px, wordmark 180 px. At smaller sizes use the symbol, or the small drawing at 16–32 px.

Preserve the proportions and supplied margins. Keep a lower-node diameter of clear space around a full logo. Dedicated small icons and tiles have fitted spacing.

Lettering is outlined custom artwork. You can edit its vector paths without installing a font. The approved 3C raster is included alongside the flattened imagegen master used for tracing.

README.txt contains the complete usage notes. asset-index.json lists dimensions and transparency. Source scripts rebuild the exports with Python 3, rsvg-convert, and ImageMagick 7.
